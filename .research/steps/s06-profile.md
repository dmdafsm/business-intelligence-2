# S06 Company profile

Status: completed. Depends on S02–S05. Part of the [MVP plan](../mvp-plan.md).

## Outcome

One page per company with general information, contact details, address, activities, status history and the full financial history as a table and charts, each value showing its source and period.

## Acceptance

- Sections: overview, contacts, activities, history, financials.
- Financial table covers every available year; missing years show as missing, not zero.
- Charts render on the server or degrade to the table without JavaScript.
- Source attribution and period are visible for every section.
